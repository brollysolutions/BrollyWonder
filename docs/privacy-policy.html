Brolly ("we," "us," or "our") provides Brolly Wonder, a children's educational application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to parents and guardians.

1. Information handled by Brolly Wonder

Information stored on the device

Brolly Wonder lets a user enter a player display name and choose an avatar. The app also records:

  • player display name and selected avatar;
  • lesson and quiz results;
  • learning progress, skill statistics, and activity history;
  • time spent on learning activities;
  • streaks, levels, stars, coins, badges, and rewards; and
  • virtual-pet selections and progress.

This information provides the learning experience, shows progress in the parent dashboard, and preserves progress between sessions. In the current version, it is stored locally on the user's device. Brolly does not require an account.

Parents and guardians should encourage children to use a nickname rather than a full legal name. The app does not ask for an email address, telephone number, precise location, photograph, contacts, microphone recording, or camera access.

Advertising and automatically processed information

Brolly Wonder includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising technology may automatically collect or process the device's IP address (which may estimate general location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID where permitted.

Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit and may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and advertising information. Brolly does not sell children's personal information.

2. How information is used

  • Operate lessons, quizzes, rewards, and virtual-pet features.
  • Save and display learning progress.
  • Provide an on-device parent or guardian dashboard.
  • Maintain reliability, security, and performance.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Prevent advertising fraud and abuse.

3. Children's privacy

Brolly Wonder is designed as an educational experience for children. We aim to minimize information requested from children and do not require account registration. A player display name is stored on the device and may be a nickname.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us. Advertising providers may process device and usage information as described above. Brolly is responsible for configuring advertising services appropriately for the declared audience and applicable child-privacy requirements.

4. Sharing of information

Brolly does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the public, or in connection with a permitted business reorganization.

5. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored player and learning information remains on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Android backup settings may include app data in backups managed by the device platform or the user's Google account.

A parent or guardian can delete local data through Android settings by clearing Brolly Wonder's storage, or by uninstalling it. Advertising information processed by Google is subject to Google's retention controls. Users can reset or delete their advertising ID through supported Android privacy settings.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by the app. Player and learning information is stored in the app's private device storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. International processing

Third-party providers such as Google may process information outside the user's country. Their processing is governed by their privacy terms and applicable legal safeguards.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, third-party services, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date above and may communicate material changes through the app or store listing.
