# Privacy Policy for Brolly Wonder

**Effective date:** August 20, 2026  
**Developer:** Brolly  
**Contact:** 8186844555

Brolly ("we," "us," or "our") provides Brolly Wonder, a children's educational application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to parents and guardians.

## 1. Information handled by Brolly Wonder

### Information stored on the device

Brolly Wonder lets a user enter a player display name and choose an avatar. The app also records learning and gameplay information, including:

- player display name and selected avatar;
- lesson and quiz results;
- learning progress, skill statistics, and activity history;
- time spent on learning activities;
- streaks, levels, stars, coins, badges, and rewards; and
- virtual-pet selections and progress.

This information is used to provide the app's learning experience, show progress in the parent dashboard, and preserve progress between sessions. In the current version, this information is stored locally on the user's device. Brolly does not require users to create an account.

Parents and guardians should encourage children to use a nickname rather than a full legal name. Brolly Wonder does not ask for an email address, telephone number, precise location, photograph, contacts, microphone recording, or camera access.

### Advertising and automatically processed information

Brolly Wonder includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising technology may automatically collect or process information such as:

- the device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
- interactions with the app and advertisements, such as app launches, taps, and video views;
- diagnostic and performance information; and
- device or account identifiers, which may include the Android advertising ID and app set ID where permitted.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google's handling of information is governed by its own policies. More information is available at:

- <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

Brolly does not sell children's personal information.

## 2. How information is used

Information handled by the app is used to:

- operate lessons, quizzes, rewards, and virtual-pet features;
- save and display learning progress;
- provide parents or guardians with an on-device progress dashboard;
- maintain app reliability, security, and performance;
- display and measure advertisements; and
- prevent advertising fraud and abuse.

## 3. Children's privacy

Brolly Wonder is designed as an educational experience for children. We aim to minimize the information requested from children and do not require account registration. A player display name is stored on the device and may be a nickname.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us using the address below. We will review the request and take reasonable steps within our control.

Advertising providers may process device and usage information as described above. Brolly is responsible for configuring advertising services appropriately for the app's declared audience and applicable child-privacy requirements.

## 4. Sharing of information

Brolly does not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the public, or in connection with a business reorganization where permitted by law.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored player and learning information remains on the device until the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Android device-backup settings may cause app data to be included in backups managed by the device platform or the user's Google account.

A parent or guardian can delete locally stored Brolly Wonder data through the Android device settings by selecting the app and clearing its storage, or by uninstalling the app. Advertising information processed by Google is subject to Google's retention controls and privacy policy. Users can reset or delete their advertising ID through supported Android privacy settings.

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, a parent or guardian may contact Brolly at the address below.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by the app. Player and learning information is stored in the app's private storage area on the device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 7. International processing

Third-party service providers such as Google may process information in countries other than the user's country. Their processing is governed by their privacy terms and applicable legal safeguards.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its third-party services, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date at the top of this policy. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or its store listing where appropriate.
